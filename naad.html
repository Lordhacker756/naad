Hold a swara. Watch it hold still.

Naad listens to your bansuri and tells you which swara you are on, how far off it is in cents, whether your breath is steady, and whether you moved cleanly to the next note.

1Allow the microphone. Audio is analysed entirely in this tab. Nothing is recorded, stored, or uploaded.
2Set your Sa. Default is a C natural bansuri. If yours differs, play your Sa once and press Tune to my Sa in Settings.
3Play. Sit about an arm's length from the mic, in a quiet room, and start with a long Sa.
